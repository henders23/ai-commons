## What you get

A guide of around 25,000 words, deliberately scoped to developing *you*: your knowledge, your reflection, your career, and your leadership of others. It is not about marking, lesson planning or resource generation, which the author argues are different jobs deserving a guide of their own.

- **Part 1, Foundations.** What the four assistants actually are, signing up for the free tiers, a five-minute first conversation, eight words of jargon and nothing more, which platform to use for what, and four ground rules. Rule one: no identifiable student data, ever. Optional pages cover routines, a personal knowledge vault, and a note-taking habit that makes the rest work.
- **Part 2, the use cases**, in five sections: staying current with research; reflection and self-improvement; learning and knowledge tools, all free; coaching and career development; and developing others.
- **Part 3, a capstone project**, which brings the pieces together into something you keep using.

Every use case follows the same pattern, so once you have done one you can do any of them: what it is and what it is for, a starter prompt to copy and fill in, step by step on each platform, what good looks like, quick fixes if it is not working, a caution box where needed, how to make it a routine, an extension activity, and where to save the output. Each is badged with the time it takes and the comfort level it assumes.

## How to use it

**If you are new to AI.** Do the suggested first session: Foundations pages F1 and F2, then Use Case 1. Forty-five minutes. You will have signed up, learned the ground rules, and completed a real piece of AI-assisted professional development.

**If you already use these tools.** Skip to the contents and pick a use case that matches the time you have. Section E, developing others, is the one most course leaders have not tried.

**With a team.** The guide works as a self-paced CPD programme. Agree one use case a fortnight, each person tries it, and the team compares notes. The four ground rules in Part 1 are worth adopting as a team before anyone starts.

## A note on currency

Platforms change quickly. Button names, menu locations and free-tier limits will move. The ideas, prompts and principles in the guide are written to outlast them.
