## What you get

**Part One: frameworks, definitions and rationale.** Why the definition of AI literacy matters, six influential accounts compared, five points of genuine disagreement between them, where they converge, why the definition differs by subject, the working definition used in the resource, and how the activity sequences are ordered. With references.

**Part Two: 100 activities by discipline**, ten for each of:

1. English, Literature and Modern Languages
2. History, Classics, Philosophy and Religious Studies
3. Sociology, Politics, Anthropology and Geography
4. Psychology and Cognitive Science
5. Economics, Business, Finance and Marketing
6. Law and Criminology
7. Medicine, Nursing and Health Sciences
8. Computer Science, Data Science and Engineering
9. Mathematics and Natural and Environmental Sciences
10. Creative Arts, Design, Media and Journalism

Within each discipline the ten activities are ordered as a teaching sequence, explained in the note that opens the section. The resource takes a broad view of the skill, from technical understanding and critical evaluation to ethics, disciplinary judgement and personal agency.

## Why it matters for EAP

EAP practitioners work with students across all of these disciplines, and increasingly inside them. The first group, English, Literature and Modern Languages, is directly about language: detecting the AI voice, translation critique, AI as a language-learning partner, and the line between editing and ghost-writing. The other nine show what AI literacy looks like in the fields our students are entering, which is the context in which their authorship will be assessed.

## How to use it

**Read Part One first**, even if you only want the activities. An activity designed against a technical definition of AI literacy looks nothing like one designed against a critical definition, and it helps to know which your department is working from.

**Pick a discipline group** that matches your students and run the sequence in order, or lift single activities into an existing scheme of work.

**With colleagues in a discipline.** The activities are a good basis for the partnership between EAP and discipline staff that the *Authorship in the Age of AI* paper argues for: choose two or three together and co-teach them.
